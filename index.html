Privacy Policy

Subly

Last updated 12 October 2026

Subly is a subscription tracker built to need as little of your data as possible.

In short: Subly does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no bank access, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Information we collect

None. Subly does not ask you to sign in and does not gather personal information such as your name, email, contacts, or location.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter lives only on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Your subscriptions — costs, dates, categories and notes
  • Your settings (currency, theme, reminders, app lock)
  • A small on-device history used to show spending changes and price rises

This data is removed when you uninstall the app. You can export it yourself (CSV or a JSON backup file); those files go only where you choose to save or share them.

Camera and bill scanning

If you use Scan bill, Subly reads the photo or PDF entirely on your device using Google's on-device ML Kit text recognition. The image and the extracted text are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The camera is used only while you are scanning, and only with your permission.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to scan a bill you choose to scan; processed on-device, never uploaded.
  • Notifications — only to remind you before a renewal, if you enable reminders.
  • Subly requests no location, contacts, or financial-account permissions.

No ads, no third-party tracking

Subly contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

Payments

An optional Pro upgrade is purchased through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; Subly never sees your payment details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
